# Proof Pack

The VCP proof pack is the public-facing evidence bundle for practical adoption.

It is intentionally conservative.
It does not fake adoption, metrics, rankings, compliance, or guarantees.

## What belongs in the proof pack

- labeled case studies;
- commands actually run;
- artifacts actually created;
- validation evidence;
- limitations and unknowns;
- explicit synthetic or sanitized boundaries.

## Labels

Use one or more of:
- `real`
- `sanitized`
- `maintainer-known`
- `synthetic`
- `template`

## What the proof pack must not do

- fabricate independent adopters;
- invent before/after metrics;
- imply SEO/GEO/AI visibility guarantees;
- hide what is still unverified.
