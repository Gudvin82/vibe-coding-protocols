# Portable Control Pack

<!-- vcp-version: v0.9.1 -->
<!-- methodology-version: v1.4 -->

Portable Control Pack is the minimum copyable VCP layer for adopters who do not want the whole repository at first.

Copy these files into your project to get the minimum VCP control layer.

This is a local portable scaffold only. It does not imply a hosted platform, registry, or autonomous execution layer.
