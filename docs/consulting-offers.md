# Consulting Offers

VCP can be packaged into three practical rollout shapes.
These are consulting/adoption playbooks, not managed services.

## VCP-Audit
- Duration: 2–3 weeks
- Goal: quick AI-development risk and readiness assessment
- Outputs: discovery summary, technical intake, first trust-check, initial risk register, first audit backlog, route recommendation, executive summary

## VCP-Pilot
- Duration: 6–8 weeks
- Goal: controlled rollout with one team or one product area
- Outputs: customer repo scaffold, agent kit setup, chosen track, PR Gate workflow, backlog operating model, sprint rhythm, pilot metrics, retrospective, scale recommendation

## VCP-Scale
- Duration: 3–6 months
- Goal: scale the operating model across multiple teams
- Outputs: rollout waves, governance reporting, internal ownership, enablement path, maturity checkpoints

## Boundary
- not a hosted governance platform
- not compliance certification
- not telemetry
- not automatic monitoring
