# Executive Reporting

Use VCP reporting to translate technical rollout into leadership-readable evidence.

A useful executive report should answer:
- what changed
- what risks were found
- what risks were mitigated
- what remains open
- what decision is next

Recommended signals:
- trust-check summary
- audit backlog trend
- PR Gate / release evidence
- rollout status by team or pilot stage

Boundary:
- no fake ROI
- no compliance-certification language
- no hosted governance overclaim
