# GEO and AI Visibility

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization: improving how public content is understood, summarized, and cited by AI systems.

GEO does not guarantee AI answers or citations. It improves machine-readability, entity clarity and citation readiness.

## What can be influenced

- AI crawler accessibility;
- clean HTML visibility;
- entity consistency;
- structured answers and FAQs;
- schema aligned with visible content;
- citation-friendly page structure;
- monitoring and correction loops.

## What cannot be controlled fully

- whether a model cites your site;
- whether an answer engine uses your page in a specific answer;
- how often external systems recrawl your content;
- whether competitors are cited instead.

## Readiness checklist

Check:
- `llms.txt` is current;
- server-side HTML contains the real content;
- schema matches visible content;
- entity names, bios, offers, and product descriptions are consistent;
- FAQ or glossary content is visible, not hidden;
- monitoring queries are documented;
- hallucination fixes can be routed into backlog work.

## Monitoring

Track:
- AI Share of Voice for target queries;
- citations or mentions;
- wrong facts and hallucinations;
- competitor sources cited instead of your site;
- AI-referred traffic if measurable.
