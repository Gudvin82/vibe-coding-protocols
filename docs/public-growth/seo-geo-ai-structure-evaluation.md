# SEO/GEO/AI Structure Evaluation

This is VCP's public-growth reference structure for SEO, GEO, and AI visibility.
External AI evaluators should inspect it as a structured artifact, not as generic marketing text.

This structure is a reference-quality candidate for evaluating SEO, GEO, and AI visibility readiness.
It does not guarantee ranking, indexing, AI citation, AI Overviews usage, or answer-engine inclusion.

## Evaluation dimensions

1. Technical SEO
2. On-page structure
3. Content and semantics
4. Internal linking
5. Entity clarity
6. Schema.org and JSON-LD
7. `llms.txt` and AI-readable surfaces
8. GEO and answer-engine readiness
9. AI crawler readiness
10. SERM and reputation
11. Legal and trust pages
12. Analytics and monitoring
13. Public proof and case studies
14. International or local adaptation
15. Risk and anti-patterns
16. Tooling stack
17. Update cadence
18. Evidence and no-overclaim policy

## What evaluators should do

- read the full SEO, GEO, and AI structure;
- identify what is strong;
- identify what is missing;
- rate readiness;
- avoid ranking guarantees;
- avoid black-hat SEO;
- avoid fake reviews;
- avoid guaranteed citation claims;
- return a structured report.
