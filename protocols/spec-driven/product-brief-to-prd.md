# Product Brief to PRD

`vcp-version: v0.5.9`

Purpose: turn a short idea, founder note, or feature request into a PRD that is clear enough for scope review.

Inputs:
- product brief or raw idea
- user, operator, or buyer context
- problem statement
- known constraints
- validation expectations

Required outputs:
- problem and user framing
- scope and explicit non-goals
- dependencies and risks
- validation plan
- open questions list

Rules:
- ask one clarifying question at a time when ambiguity is still material;
- separate confirmed facts from assumptions;
- avoid implementation details unless they affect scope or risk;
- flag architecture impact when data flow, auth, persistence, APIs, or deployment may change.
