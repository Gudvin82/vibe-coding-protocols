# Public Growth

Public Growth is the VCP route family for public-facing websites, docs, product pages, service pages, landing pages, blogs, marketplaces, and personal expert sites.

It extends existing Public Site Readiness with:
- technical SEO;
- GEO and AI visibility;
- entity authority;
- page templates;
- content operations;
- analytics and monitoring;
- reputation and anti-pattern control.

Use it for:
- founders and solo builders;
- agencies and studios;
- SaaS landing pages;
- documentation sites;
- AI-built public products;
- personal expert sites.

It does not guarantee:
- rankings;
- AI citations;
- lead growth;
- legal compliance.

## Relation to other routes

- New project: use Public Growth before launching public pages.
- Existing project: use Public Growth during hardening or public readiness.
- Production project: use Public Growth with analytics, monitoring, and legal review.
- Site with AI visibility goals: use GEO and AI visibility guidance.
- Content-heavy project: use Content Ops and the page template system.
