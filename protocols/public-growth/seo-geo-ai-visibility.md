# SEO, GEO, and AI Visibility

Use this protocol when a public project needs search readiness, AI-answer readability, machine-readable entity signals, and explicit ownership boundaries.

## Legend

- `[U]` Universal
- `[RU]` Russia / CIS
- `[INT]` International
- `[AI/GEO]` AI search / generative engine visibility
- `[OPT]` Optional

## Focus areas

### 1. Business and page intent `[U]`
- target audience, JTBD, objections, offers;
- page intent by TOFU / MOFU / BOFU;
- KPI and conversion target per page cluster.

### 2. Technical visibility `[U]`
- crawlability, canonical, redirects, sitemap, robots;
- SSR/SSG or equivalent HTML visibility;
- CWV, mobile, image SEO, accessibility, security basics.

### 3. Regional / market specifics `[RU]` `[INT]`
- Yandex Webmaster, Metrika, regionality, commercial factors, Yandex Business, Maps, 2GIS;
- hreflang, localization quality, Google/Bing/regional webmaster tools, local trust signals.

### 4. AI/GEO readiness `[AI/GEO]`
- important content visible without JavaScript;
- AI bots not blocked accidentally;
- `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and `ai.txt` aligned;
- entity authority and consistent brand wording;
- answer-ready sections, FAQ, glossary, structured data;
- AI Share of Voice and citation-source monitoring;
- hallucination detection and correction path.

### 5. Governance `[U]`
- RACI for SEO, content, development, analytics, PR/SERM;
- Definition of Done for pages, SEO tasks, and AI/GEO tasks;
- backlog path for errors, hallucinations, and stale content.

## Minimum artifacts

Inspect or prepare:
- `robots.txt`;
- `sitemap.xml`;
- `llms.txt`;
- canonical page structure;
- visible FAQ or glossary content;
- Schema.org aligned to visible content;
- analytics and query monitoring plan;
- ownership / RACI note;
- Definition of Done checklist.

## Do not overclaim

GEO does not guarantee rankings, AI answers, or citations.
It improves readability, structure, discoverability, and citation readiness.
