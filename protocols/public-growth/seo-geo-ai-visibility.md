# SEO, GEO, and AI Visibility

Use this protocol when a public project needs search readiness, AI-answer readability, and clearer machine-readable entity signals.

## Focus areas

- technical crawlability;
- clean HTML visibility;
- entity consistency;
- structured data that matches visible content;
- answer-ready page formatting;
- AI citation monitoring;
- hallucination detection.

## Do not overclaim

GEO does not guarantee rankings, AI answers, or citations.
It improves readability, structure, and citation readiness.

## Minimum artifacts

Inspect or prepare:
- `robots.txt`;
- `sitemap.xml`;
- `llms.txt`;
- canonical page structure;
- visible FAQ or glossary content;
- Schema.org aligned to visible content;
- analytics and query monitoring plan.
