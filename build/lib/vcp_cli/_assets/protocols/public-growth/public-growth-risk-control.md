# Public Growth Risk Control

Use this page to keep public growth work ethical, reviewable, and aligned with VCP boundaries.

## Explicitly avoid

- mass AI content without human review;
- spam links;
- fake reviews;
- hidden FAQ schema;
- misleading schema;
- doorway pages;
- accidentally blocking AI bots or search bots;
- contradictory entity facts across pages;
- legal claims without lawyer review;
- fake AI visibility claims;
- behavioral factor manipulation.

## Review triggers

Escalate when:
- structured data diverges from visible content;
- the page makes health, legal, financial, or security claims;
- analytics are missing and claims about growth are still being made;
- content is clearly thin, duplicated, or generated without real editorial value.
