# Public Growth Playbook

Use this playbook when an AI-assisted project has a public surface that must be discoverable, trustworthy, machine-readable, and commercially useful.

## 1. Strategy and baseline

Start with:
- an SEO baseline audit;
- technical errors and indexing blockers;
- penalties or filter checks where relevant;
- search competitors;
- AI-answer competitors;
- a prioritized backlog;
- current baseline metrics before changes.

Track 3, 6, and 12 month targets for:
- organic traffic;
- rankings;
- branded searches;
- conversions from organic;
- AI Share of Voice;
- AI citations;
- AI-referred traffic if measurable.

## 2. Tech stack and site architecture

Document:
- CMS or framework;
- SSR, SSG, or client-heavy rendering;
- hosting, CDN, staging, backups, and CI;
- URL structure;
- page types;
- TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU funnel coverage;
- page creation priority;
- route and page ownership.

## 3. Technical SEO

Cover:
- `robots.txt`;
- `sitemap.xml`;
- IndexNow where relevant;
- crawl budget discipline;
- redirects;
- 404 monitoring;
- Core Web Vitals;
- SSR and rendering quality;
- image SEO;
- Schema.org;
- mobile behavior;
- HTTPS and security headers;
- accessibility and WCAG.

## 4. On-page and content

Plan around:
- semantic core;
- search intent;
- `title`, description, and H1 quality;
- internal linking;
- answer-ready formatting;
- TL;DR blocks;
- FAQ formatting;
- glossary definitions;
- content clusters;
- topical authority;
- E-E-A-T signals;
- update cadence.

## 5. GEO and AI visibility

Check:
- AI crawler accessibility;
- `llms.txt`;
- `llms-full.txt` as optional extended map;
- `ai.txt` as optional helper;
- content visibility in server-side HTML;
- entity authority;
- structured answers;
- AI citation monitoring;
- AI hallucination monitoring;
- AI Share of Voice;
- branded anchor strategy.

## 6. Off-page and reputation

Track:
- brand mentions;
- digital PR;
- guest posts;
- community or industry citations;
- reviews;
- SERM;
- hallucination monitoring for brand reputation.

## 7. CRO and user behavior

Review:
- funnel analysis;
- CTAs;
- forms;
- calculators or quizzes;
- UX friction;
- heatmaps where available;
- A/B testing where appropriate.

## 8. Legal and compliance

Check:
- privacy policy;
- terms and offers;
- cookie consent;
- AI-generated content policy where relevant;
- local legal review warnings;
- accessibility legal requirements by region.

## 9. Analytics and monitoring

Set up and verify:
- Google Search Console and GA4;
- Yandex Webmaster and Metrika;
- Bing Webmaster Tools;
- AI bot log visibility if available;
- AI citation tracking;
- dashboards;
- weekly and monthly reporting.

## 10. Risks and anti-patterns

Do not rely on:
- mass AI thin content;
- doorway or programmatic pages without unique value;
- spam links;
- behavioral manipulation;
- duplicate content without canonical control;
- indexable junk pages;
- blocking AI bots accidentally;
- broken analytics due to consent logic;
- contradictory content that amplifies LLM hallucinations;
- unsupported legal, medical, or financial claims.

## Outcome

The output should be a prioritized Public Growth backlog that separates:
- must-fix technical blockers;
- page and content gaps;
- AI visibility gaps;
- reputation risks;
- analytics and reporting gaps;
- legal review dependencies.
